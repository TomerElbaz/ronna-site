## Invite codes

- When you confirm your place, you may get invite codes to share with people
  you know. Each code works once, within 30 days.
- A code doesn't move anyone up the list, you included, and it earns no reward.
- We don't send codes to anyone. You share them yourself, if you want to.
- You can cancel your unused codes at any time with the link in any email from
  us.
- Please don't post codes publicly or sell them. We may cancel codes that are
  misused.

## Leaving the list

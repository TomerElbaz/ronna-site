This page covers the MOM waitlist only. MOM itself will have its own privacy
notice when it opens.

## What we collect

- **Your email address.**
- **Two dates:** when you joined, and when you confirmed.
- **If someone invited you:** while your invite is waiting for you to confirm, a
  note of which invite code you used. Once you confirm, that note is removed
  for codes shared by other people on the list. We never store the name of the
  person who invited you.
- **If you're on the list and get invite codes to share:** a scrambled form of
  each code (we can't read the code back), and whether it has been used. Never
  who used it.

That's all. No name, no phone number, no home address, no cookies, no
tracking, no device details.

To stop abuse, we count sign-ups using a scrambled form of your internet
address that changes every day. We never store the address itself, and each
day's counts are deleted the next day.

## Why

To tell you when MOM is ready for you. Nothing else.

## How long we keep it

- **If you never confirm:** your address is deleted after 7 days.
- **If you confirm:** we keep it until you leave the list. **[to confirm:** do
  we also delete the list once invites have gone out, and when?**]**
- **Backups:** our database keeps backup copies for up to 30 days, so a deleted
  address can stay in a backup for up to 30 days before it is gone.
  **[to confirm:** 30 days matches the Cloudflare plan in use**]**

## How to leave the list

- Open the link in any email from us and press **Delete**. Your address is gone
  at once.
- Or go to **ronna.mom/off**, enter your email, and we'll send you that link.
- Or reply to any email from us asking to leave. We'll remove your address by
  hand within 7 days.

## Who handles it

Two companies handle your address for us, each under its own privacy terms:

- **Cloudflare** runs this website and stores the list.
  Privacy terms: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
- **Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES)** sends our emails, from its US East
  (Ohio) region. Privacy terms: aws.amazon.com/privacy

Nobody else gets your address.

## What we never do

- We never sell, rent or share your address.
- We never send you marketing, and we never add you to any other list.
- The only emails we send are the one that confirms your place, a link to
  leave the list when you ask for one, and the one that says MOM is ready for
  you. **[to confirm:** the "MOM is ready" email is not built yet**]**
- Our emails never carry tracking pixels or rewritten links.
- We never use your address to train any model.

What MOM is

MOM is the console for your household. MOM is the console for your business.

MOM keeps one record of your household: the schedule, the bills, the people who work for you and the agents that act for you. Eight desks each watch one part of the house and bring you only what needs a decision. Nothing happens until you say Go.

MOM keeps one record of your business: the schedule, vendors, bills and staff. Each desk watches one part of the work and brings you only what needs a decision. Nothing happens until someone you named says Go.

Pricing comes with your invite. The first business pilots run in Austin.

What MOM never does

  • Nothing about your family is used to train any model.
  • Passwords and card numbers are never stored in MOM.
  • Nothing leaves the house without your Go.
  • The day you disconnect, your family's data is deleted from MOM. Backup copies are gone within 30 days.
  • Nothing about your business is used to train any model.
  • Passwords and card numbers are never stored in MOM.
  • Nothing leaves the business without a Go from someone you named.
  • The day you disconnect, your business's data is deleted from MOM. Backup copies are gone within 30 days.

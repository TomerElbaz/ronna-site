What MOM is

MOM is the console for your household.

MOM keeps one record of your household: the schedule, the bills, the people who work for you and the agents that act for you. Eight desks each watch one part of the house and bring you only what needs a decision. Nothing goes out in your name until you say Go.

What MOM never does

  • Nothing about your family is used to train any model.
  • MOM never asks for your passwords or card numbers, and removes them when it finds them.
  • Nothing goes out in your name without your Go.
  • The day you disconnect, your family's data is deleted from MOM. Backup copies are gone within 30 days.

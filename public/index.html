What MOM never does

  • Nothing about your family is used to train any model.
  • MOM never asks for your passwords or card numbers, and removes them when it finds them.
  • Nothing goes out in your name without your Go.
  • The day you disconnect, your family's data is deleted from MOM. Backup copies are gone within 30 days.

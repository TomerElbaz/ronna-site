MOM waitlist

Privacy

This page covers the MOM waitlist only. MOM itself will have its own privacy notice when it opens.

What we collect

Your email address, and the dates you joined and confirmed. Nothing else: no name, no phone, no address, no tracking.

Why

To tell you when MOM is ready for you, and nothing else.

How to leave the list

Use the link in any email from us; it removes your address at once. Or reply to any of our emails and we'll remove it by hand within 7 days.

Take me off the list

Never

  • We never sell or share your address.
  • We never add you to any other list.
  • Our emails never carry tracking pixels or rewritten links.
  • We never use your address to train any model.

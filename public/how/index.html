How MOM works

How it works at home. How it works at work.

You tell MOM what's going on. MOM puts it in the right place and drafts what comes next. You decide.

Your team tells MOM what's going on. MOM puts it in the right place and drafts what comes next. You decide.

Three steps

  1. 01

    Step 1: Tell MOM

    Say it or type it as you would to a person: "The plumber can come Thursday."

    Say it, type it or forward the email: "The supplier moved delivery to Friday."

  2. 02

    Step 2: MOM files it

    MOM puts it on the right desk and the right person's track, and asks one question only when she can't tell.

    MOM puts it on the right desk and the right person's lane, and asks one question only when she can't tell.

  3. 03

    Step 3: You say Go

    Anything that leaves the house waits for your Go: a reply, a booking, a call. You can always say No or Edit.

    Anything that leaves the business waits for a Go from someone you named. No and Edit are always there.

We open in small groups. Save your household a place. The first pilots run in Austin. Save your business a place.

How MOM works

One chat runs the house.

You tell MOM what's going on, in words or a photo. MOM takes it to the right desk, answers from what your household already knows, and turns anything to do or pay into one line. You say Go.

Five steps

  1. 01

    Step 1: Say it or snap it

    Type it, say it, or take a photo: a receipt, a letter from school, the label on the water heater.

  2. 02

    Step 2: MOM sends it to the right desk

    Each part of the house has its own desk. Eight of them. The appointment goes to the one for health; the receipt to the one for bills.

  3. 03

    Step 3: The desk answers from what you already know

    It checks what your household has already told MOM, so you don't have to say it twice.

  4. 04

    Step 4: One line, then Go

    Anything to do or pay becomes one line. Nothing goes out in your name until you say Go. You can always say No or Edit.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Everything stays findable

    Ask later and MOM finds it: the line, the photo, and when you said Go.
